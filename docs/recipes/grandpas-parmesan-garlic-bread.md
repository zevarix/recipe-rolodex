# Grandpa's Parmesan Garlic Bread

<p class="recipe-intro">A generously buttered rustic Italian loaf with garlic and Parmesan, baked until the middle stays soft and the edges turn golden.</p>

<div class="recipe-facts">
  <span><b>Serves</b> 4–6</span>
  <span><b>Prep</b> 5 min</span>
  <span><b>Bake</b> 10–12 min</span>
  <span><b>Oven</b> 400°F</span>
</div>

## Ingredients

- **1 rustic Italian loaf** (use half if the loaf is unusually large)
- **5 tbsp softened butter**
- **3–4 cloves garlic**, minced
- **2 tbsp grated Parmesan**, including Kraft-style shaker Parmesan
- **Optional:** Chopped parsley or a pinch of black pepper

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to **400°F**.
2. Split the loaf lengthwise into two broad halves. Place cut sides up on a baking sheet.
3. In a small bowl, thoroughly combine the softened butter, minced garlic, and Parmesan.
4. Spread the garlic butter generously over the cut surfaces all the way to the edges.
5. Bake for **10–12 minutes** until warmed through with golden edges.
6. For a crispier top, broil for **30–60 seconds** while watching continuously. Slice and serve hot.

## Kitchen notes

- **Soft and buttery:** Don't broil; pull it once the middle is hot.
- **Crispier:** Let the edges brown a little longer, then finish with a brief broil.
- **If Grandpa's garlic is missing:** A spoonful of prepared basil pesto mixed into the butter can make an emergency garlic-herb bread. Reduce added salt.
- **Allergens:** Contains wheat and dairy; commercial pesto may also contain nuts.
