# Shrimp Pesto Supper

<p class="recipe-intro">A complete dinner for 4–6: a small bowl of New England clam chowder, garlic-butter shrimp pesto spaghetti, and Grandpa's golden garlic bread.</p>

<div class="recipe-facts">
  <span><b>Serves</b> 4–6</span>
  <span><b>Total</b> 40–50 min</span>
  <span><b>Oven</b> 400°F</span>
  <span><b>Style</b> Seafood supper</span>
</div>

<div class="recipe-note"><strong>Menu, not a homemade chowder recipe:</strong> The starter uses refrigerated <em>Clam Shack New England Clam Chowder</em> sold by Costco, in a two-pack of 24 oz tubs. Prepare it according to its package directions.</div>

## The menu

1. **Starter:** New England clam chowder, about ½ cup per guest.
2. **Main:** [Garlic-butter shrimp pesto spaghetti](../recipes/garlic-butter-shrimp-pesto-spaghetti.md), using 2 lb shrimp and 17.6 oz spaghetti.
3. **Side:** [Grandpa's Parmesan garlic bread](../recipes/grandpas-parmesan-garlic-bread.md), made from a rustic Italian loaf.

## Shopping and prep list

- 1 full 17.6 oz package spaghetti
- 2 lb raw, peeled and deveined jumbo shrimp
- ¾–1 cup basil pesto; 3 tbsp butter; 1 tbsp olive oil; 4 garlic cloves
- 1 rustic Italian loaf; 5 tbsp softened butter; 3–4 more cloves garlic; 2 tbsp Parmesan
- **1 refrigerated 24 oz container of chowder to start**, giving six ½-cup appetizer portions; warm part or all of a second tub if guests want larger bowls
- Black pepper; salt for pasta water; optional lemon juice

## Cooking timeline

1. **0–15 min · Prep.** Peel and dry the shrimp; mince garlic; mix and spread Grandpa's garlic butter. Heat oven to 400°F.
2. **15–25 min · Appetizer.** Warm refrigerated chowder according to its package directions, stirring as directed. Reheat thoroughly to 165°F. Serve small bowls.
3. **20–35 min · Main underway.** Bring salted pasta water to a boil, then cook spaghetti to al dente. Save 1 cup pasta water before draining.
4. **25–40 min · Bread in oven.** Bake cut-side up at 400°F for 10–12 minutes, aiming for a hot finish near serving time.
5. **30–45 min · Shrimp.** Sear in two or three batches in butter and olive oil. Add garlic late to avoid burning; cook to 145°F internally.
6. **40–50 min · Combine and serve.** Off the heat, toss pasta with ¾ cup pesto and enough reserved water to make it silky; add the shrimp. Serve with hot bread.

## Three things that make it work

- Don't crowd the shrimp skillet.
- Don't overcook the shrimp or the pesto.
- Bring the garlic bread out hot, just as the pasta is ready.

!!! note "Keep food safe"
    Keep chilled shrimp and chowder refrigerated until use. Check the chowder's printed use-by date, storage instructions, and allergen information. The packaged starter may contain shellfish, fish, milk, and other allergens; the pasta meal includes shrimp and may contain nuts/dairy through pesto.
