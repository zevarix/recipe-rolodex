## The menu

1. **Starter:** New England clam chowder, about ½ cup per guest.
2. **Main:** [Garlic-butter shrimp pesto spaghetti](../recipes/garlic-butter-shrimp-pesto-spaghetti.md), with the shrimp and spaghetti amounts shown in the main recipe.
3. **Side:** [Grandpa's Parmesan garlic bread](../recipes/grandpas-parmesan-garlic-bread.md), made from a rustic Italian loaf.

## Shopping and prep list

- <span class="recipe-amount" data-base="1" data-unit="package">1 package</span> (<span class="recipe-amount" data-base="17.6" data-unit="oz" data-format="decimal1">17.6 oz</span>) spaghetti; Garofalo packs are 500 g each
- <span class="recipe-amount" data-base="2" data-unit="lb">2 lb</span> raw, peeled and deveined jumbo shrimp
- <span class="recipe-amount" data-base="0.75" data-max="1" data-unit="cup">¾–1 cup</span> basil pesto; <span class="recipe-amount" data-base="3" data-unit="tbsp">3 tbsp</span> butter; <span class="recipe-amount" data-base="1" data-unit="tbsp">1 tbsp</span> olive oil; <span class="recipe-amount" data-base="4" data-unit="clove" data-plural="cloves" data-format="whole">4 cloves</span> garlic
- <span class="recipe-amount" data-base="1" data-unit="loaf" data-plural="loaves">1 loaf</span> rustic Italian bread; <span class="recipe-amount" data-base="5" data-unit="tbsp">5 tbsp</span> softened butter; <span class="recipe-amount" data-base="3" data-max="4" data-unit="clove" data-plural="cloves" data-format="whole">3–4 cloves</span> more garlic; <span class="recipe-amount" data-base="2" data-unit="tbsp">2 tbsp</span> Parmesan
- <span class="recipe-amount" data-base="3" data-unit="cup">3 cups</span> refrigerated chowder (½ cup per guest), sold in 24 oz tubs; open enough tubs to cover the selected servings
- Black pepper; salt for pasta water; optional lemon juice

## Cooking timeline

1. **0–15 min · Prep.** Peel and dry the shrimp; mince garlic; mix and spread Grandpa's garlic butter. Heat oven to 400°F.
2. **15–25 min · Appetizer.** Warm refrigerated chowder according to its package directions, stirring as directed. Reheat thoroughly to 165°F. Serve small bowls.
3. **20–35 min · Main underway.** Bring salted pasta water to a boil, then cook spaghetti to al dente. Save some hot pasta water before draining.
4. **25–40 min · Bread in oven.** Bake cut-side up at 400°F for 10–12 minutes, aiming for a hot finish near serving time.
5. **30–45 min · Shrimp.** Sear in batches as needed in butter and olive oil. Add garlic late to avoid burning; cook to 145°F internally.
6. **40–50 min · Combine and serve.** Off the heat, toss pasta with the measured pesto and enough reserved water to make it silky; add the shrimp. Serve with hot bread.

## Three things that make it work

- Don't crowd the shrimp skillet.
- Don't overcook the shrimp or the pesto.
- Bring the garlic bread out hot, just as the pasta is ready.

!!! note "Keep food safe"
    Keep chilled shrimp and chowder refrigerated until use. Check the chowder's printed use-by date, storage instructions, and allergen information. The packaged starter may contain shellfish, fish, milk, and other allergens; the pasta meal includes shrimp and may contain nuts/dairy through pesto.
