## The menu

1. **Main:** [Cowboy Butter pan-seared cod](../recipes/cowboy-butter-pan-seared-cod.md), basted with shallot-and-herb seasoning.
2. **Roasted sides:** Butternut squash, broccoli florets, and sliced yellow onion, lightly seasoned with the shallot-and-herb blend.
3. **Quick side:** Asparagus sautéed in butter after the cod comes out of the pan.

## Shopping and prep list

The weights below reflect the **approximate Costco produce packs** we planned around, not an exact record of how much was cooked that night. Use what fits on your roasting pans, and save any surplus for another meal.

- <span class="recipe-amount" data-base="6" data-unit="portion" data-format="whole">6 portions</span> of **cod**, cut from a large fillet (exact weight not recorded)
- Approximately <span class="recipe-amount" data-base="2" data-unit="lb">2 lb</span> **pre-chopped butternut squash**
- Up to <span class="recipe-amount" data-base="3" data-unit="lb">3 lb</span> **broccoli florets**, depending on space and appetites
- Up to <span class="recipe-amount" data-base="2.25" data-unit="lb">2¼ lb</span> **asparagus**, tough ends trimmed
- <span class="recipe-amount" data-base="1" data-unit="onion">1 onion</span>, **yellow**, sliced
- **Butter and olive oil**, as needed for the roasting pans and skillet
- **Shallot-and-herb seasoning** for vegetables and cod, to taste
- **Kinder's Cowboy Butter seasoning**, to taste, mostly for the cod and asparagus
- **Salt and black pepper**, as needed

## Cooking timeline

1. **0–10 min · Start the squash and onions.** Preheat the oven to **425°F**. Toss chopped butternut squash and sliced onion with a little olive oil and shallot-and-herb seasoning. Spread out on a large sheet pan and start roasting.
2. **10–15 min · Add the broccoli.** Toss broccoli florets lightly with oil, shallot seasoning, and any needed salt. Spread on a separate pan (or use additional pans) so the vegetables roast rather than steam. Put in the oven about **10–15 minutes after the squash**.
3. **15–35 min · Let the vegetables roast.** Turn them once if needed. The squash should become tender and lightly browned; broccoli should develop browned edges. The exact time depends on piece size and how crowded the pans are.
4. **25–45 min · Sear the cod.** Pat the fish dry and cut it into portions. Sear in butter and oil in batches: about **3 minutes on the first side**, then flip, lower heat, season with shallot-and-herb, and butter-baste a couple more minutes. Finish lightly with Cowboy Butter. Follow the [full cod method](../recipes/cowboy-butter-pan-seared-cod.md) and check for **145°F** in the thickest piece.
5. **Last 3–5 min · Sauté asparagus.** Transfer finished cod to a warm plate. In the same pan, cook the trimmed asparagus with a little fresh butter and a touch of Cowboy Butter seasoning, stirring until bright green and tender-crisp. Use batches if your skillet is small.
6. **Serve together.** Put the roasted vegetables in separate dishes, bring out the cod with its pan butter, and serve the asparagus hot.

*The 40–50-minute timeline was our original plan; a particularly large vegetable haul or extra fish batches can take longer.*

## What makes it work

- **Use two vegetable pans or cook in batches.** Squash needs an earlier start, while broccoli benefits from a hotter, less crowded surface.
- **Don't cook all the cod at once** if pieces overlap or cool the skillet.
- **Save asparagus for last.** That lets it pick up the buttery skillet flavor without getting soggy.
- **Keep seasoning flexible.** The two blends provide most of the flavor; taste before adding more salt.
- **Scale with judgment.** The ingredient quantities follow the original grocery-pack plan. Servings change the displayed estimates, but roasting space, number of pans, and cooking time must be adjusted separately.

!!! note "Food safety"
    Keep raw cod cold until cooking. Wash hands, utensils, and surfaces after handling raw fish. Cook the thickest cod portion to **145°F**. Check both seasoning packages for allergens and sodium.
